In Mask-A-Raid, you play as a father separated from his family during a catastrophic outbreak. The city is locked down, crawling with the infected, and patrolled by a ruthless police force. To survive, you cannot just fight; you must adapt.

The core of survival is the Mask System. Every enemy you defeat offers a chance to steal their identity. Wear a Police mask to pass checkpoints unnoticed, or a Zombie mask to walk among the dead. But be warned: every disguise has its limits, and the city is watching your every move.

Mechanics:
W/A/S/D - to move
long press E - to pick up
shift - run
ctrl - crouch
left click - use an item

The Mask-A-Raid System:
Police Mask: Grants immunity to police detection at checkpoints.
Zombie Mask: Allows you to move freely among the infected—just don't get too close.
Doctor Mask: Hides you from medical personnel.
The Official's Mask: The ultimate disguise. Obtain this to become invisible to the Government, Police, and Nurses simultaneously.
Tactical Zombie Evasion:
Zombies react to sight and sound. Never walk directly in front of them, or they will grab you and scream, attracting a horde.
Keep your distance and strike from the shadows.
The Doctor's Dilemma:
Doctors are the only ones who can heal your infection, but they are a double-edged sword.
One Doctor is a lifeline, though they leave you vulnerable to physical attacks from zombies.
Two Doctors is a death sentence. Their jealousy is fatal—getting greedy for healing will get you killed by the very people meant to save you.
The Scorched Earth Event:
Calculated actions are key. If you cause unnecessary chaos or fail too many stealth checks, you will trigger "Scorched Earth."
Once active, every enemy in the sector knows your location and will hunt you down. Only the rare Government Official Mask can nullify this state.
